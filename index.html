Sides Helper

The sides tool and the read-along in one app, for the sound cart. iPad and Mac.

Sides Helper opens the day's sides, shows every character in a highlighter color, and prints the enlarged pages the same way the free web tool does. With Follow on, it listens to the feed and the page follows the take, showing who is on now and who is next with the source you typed.

The free web tool is at gotham-sound.github.io/sides-enlarger.
