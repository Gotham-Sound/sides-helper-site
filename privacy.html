Sides Helper Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-21. Gotham Sound and Communications, Inc., New York.

What the app collects: nothing.

Sides Helper has no account, no server and no analytics. The app does not send your scripts, your audio, your notes, your source assignments or your settings anywhere. They stay on your iPad or Mac.

The script.

The PDF you open is parsed on the device and kept on the device so the app reopens where you left off. Close packet removes it. Forget everything on this device removes it along with the KEY and every setting.

Audio.

With Follow on, the app listens to the audio input you chose. Each few seconds of audio is recognized on the device and discarded. The recognized words move the page and are not stored or shown. Nothing is recorded.

The camera.

The Picture display shows a camera connected over USB behind the cue belt. The video is shown and nothing more: not recorded, not stored, not sent.

Network use.

The app uses the network in one case: buying the Follow unlock goes through Apple's App Store on the iPad. The speech model ships inside the app, so the first run downloads nothing. Only if you pick the larger small or medium model in Settings does the app fetch that one model once from its host, Hugging Face; that request carries what any download carries, your network address, and nothing about you or your script. On the Mac, the license key is checked on the Mac itself; there is no check-in.

Reports you send.

Settings > Send a report opens an email from your own mail account to support@gothamsound.com with a small attachment: the app version, your device model, your feed and display settings, and the app's own logs, in which words are hashed. It never includes the script, the audio or your notes. You see the message before it goes and you can remove the attachment. We keep reports to fix what you reported.

Release notes.

After buying, the app offers to email us so we can tell you about new versions. That is an email you send; we keep the address for release notes only and remove it when you ask.

Children.

Sides Helper is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes.

Changes to this policy appear on this page with a new effective date.
