Sides Helper Support

For the iPad and Mac app from Gotham Sound.

Write to support@gothamsound.com. A sentence about what you expected and what happened is plenty.

The manual

The user manual covers both the iPad and the Mac: install, opening the sides, the roster and the KEY, the feed, Follow and its displays, what the colors mean, exports, privacy and troubleshooting.

A bug in the app

Settings, in the app's ⋯ menu, has Send a report (bug or idea). It opens an email from your own mail account with a small diagnostic file attached: the app version, the device, the feed and display settings, and the app's logs with every word hashed. It never carries the script, the audio or your notes, and you see the message before it goes.

A PDF that will not open

Open sample sides on the first screen opens a short made-up scene. If that opens and yours does not, send the report and say where the file lives: a folder that syncs through iCloud, a network share, or the device itself.

The Mac version

The disk image carries a Read me first with the install steps, including the one-time Open Anyway step for a build that is not yet signed with an Apple developer certificate.

Follow

Follow runs free for 14 days from the first time it is switched on. After that it is a one-time unlock: through the App Store on the iPad, and with a key from Gotham Sound on the Mac.
