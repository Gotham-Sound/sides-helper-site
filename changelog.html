Sides Helper Changelog

One entry per build that reached testers, newest first. The build number is in Settings on both platforms and in the Mac disk image's Read me first. The same page is on the site at https://gotham-sound.github.io/sides-helper-site/changelog.html.

Build 181, 2026-09-26 (iPad and Mac)

  • A call sheet or a coverage grid in front of the sides is left alone as a whole page: nothing on it enlarges or lands in the character list, and the Print / Export screen names the page it left out.
  • Forget everything turns Follow off before it clears the device, and the Settings text says what it removes. The trial clock stays.
  • Check the feed has no speech model picker any more. The app runs the model it ships with and never downloads one; the Speech section keeps the window and the counters.
  • On the iPad, the unlock price appears once the App Store has answered, in your own currency. If Apple refunds the unlock, Follow locks again; a moment without the App Store never locks it. On the Mac, the license screen says once per person, and Open license file looks for .sideshelper-license files.
  • Under the hood: audio buffers reach the listener in the order they arrived, on every input, and the app is built with Swift 6's concurrency checking on, so the compiler now checks every handoff between the audio thread, the speech engine and the page. Old report folders are cleared from the temporary folder a day after Mail took them.

Build 174, 2026-09-25 (iPad and Mac)

  • Display is shorter: ten of our own switches are gone and their values are set the way the app already ran. What is left is yours: theme, layout, script, names, text size, the Follow display, Ahead of the ears, page movement, follow position, after you scroll, Stay in the scene, how many names the rail shows, note size and the Pencil.
  • Picture, the camera view, is out for now. Page, Drum and Horizon stay.
  • Plain names: Feed + STT check is Check the feed; the tray of notes that could not find their line is called that. The off-script gate and the far-jump hold are gone from Settings; they run at the values the measurements chose.
  • Send a report has its own line in the ⋯ menu next to Settings, and on the Mac in the app menu. Settings opens nothing else. The email screen after an unlock is gone.
  • The bar during a take: the Follow button says Follow, the pill alone shows the state, and the scene-lock capsule appears only while a scene is locked (Display > Follow > Stay in the scene, or the Follow menu on the Mac, locks it; a tap on the capsule releases it).
  • From here on, builds go out on Mondays unless something is broken.

Build 161, 2026-09-24 (Mac)

  • File > Close (Cmd-W) closes the sides and keeps the window. The Close Window item that 152 added is gone: the red dot does that, and any menu item brings the window back. Open a sides link is in the File menu now.
  • A Follow menu in the menu bar, with keys: Follow (Cmd-F), Hold (Cmd-Shift-H), Stay in the scene (Cmd-Shift-L), Top of scene (Cmd-Up), Next scene (Cmd-Down), Scenes (Cmd-L) and Feed + STT check. Every item in the ⋯ menu has a home in the menu bar now, a tester's ask.
  • Character names stay out of a report unless you tick Include character names on the report sheet, as in 157 on the iPad. Unticked, the zip says NAME 1, NAME 2 and so on in their place.

Build 157, 2026-09-24 (iPad)

  • Character names stay out of a report unless you tick Include character names on the report sheet. Unticked, the zip says NAME 1, NAME 2 and so on in their place, in every file, and the KEY's sources and colors still go. The character list is part of the script, so it goes only when you say so.
  • Two doors fewer, as in 152 on the Mac: Close packet is gone from the bottom of the Roster and KEY sheet (it is in the ⋯ menu), and the Display link is gone from Settings (Display is the sliders button on the page). Feed + STT check and Send a report stay in Settings.

Build 152, 2026-09-24 (Mac)

  • Cmd-W is Close packet: it closes the sides and the window stays, on the empty screen with Open PDF. Close Window is Cmd-Shift-W. Before, Cmd-W was macOS's own Close Window, and the app kept running with nothing on screen: the File menu still worked, the Open panel still came up, and the PDF you picked opened into a window that was not there. That is the File > Open that "did nothing" on one tester's Mac since build 110, found from his video. Every menu item that shows something now brings the window back first, and so does a click on the Dock icon, so a closed window is never a dead end.
  • Two doors fewer, both platforms in the next iPad build: Close packet is gone from the bottom of the Roster and KEY sheet (it is in the ⋯ menu, and on the Mac in the File menu), and the Display link is gone from Settings (Display is the sliders button on the page; with no sides open there is nothing for it to show). Feed + STT check and Send a report stay in Settings.
  • The first image signed by Gotham Sound and Communications, Inc.: the Developer ID and the notarization are the company's now. Nothing else changed since 139.

Build 142, 2026-09-24 (iPad)

  • The first build from Gotham Sound and Communications' own developer account. It arrives through a new TestFlight invitation; the old one keeps working until it is retired. Settings on the iPad start fresh once, the same way the Mac's did, because the app carries Gotham's identifier now. Nothing else changed since 139.

Build 139, 2026-09-23

  • Send a report works from Settings, on both platforms. Settings closes and the report sheet opens. Before, a second sheet asked for while Settings was up was dropped without a word, so Send a report, Display and Feed + STT check did nothing when opened from Settings, and Import .sceneline did nothing from Print / Export. All four open now, the sheet they came from closing first. The register screen after an unlock opens over Settings.

Build 138, 2026-09-23

  • A report cannot die on one bad part. Each file in the zip is written on its own; a part that fails becomes a short error file inside the zip and the rest still goes to Mail. A report that cannot be built at all says so in plain words on the sheet instead of a small caption that reads as nothing.

Build 137, 2026-09-23

  • Send a report always leaves you with the zip. The sheet now says what Mail actually did, and it has a button that shows the zip in the Finder on the Mac, or opens the share sheet with it on the iPad, so a report can go by any mail, Messages or AirDrop when Mail opens nothing. Before, the sheet said Mail had opened whether it had or not, and the zip sat in a folder nobody could find.

Build 136, 2026-09-23 (Mac)

  • The scene list, held open from either arrow, shows its scenes on the Mac. In 131 the sheet came up as a title with nothing under it.
  • Settings start fresh with this build on the Mac: the input pick, Display and the KEY memory. The app now carries Gotham Sound's identifier, and macOS keeps an app's settings under its identifier. This happens once.

Build 131, 2026-09-23

  • The up arrow steps back through scenes: one tap is Top of scene, as before; at the top, a tap goes to the scene before, and again for the one before that, for a run of short scenes shot together. A new down arrow beside it goes to the next scene. Hold either arrow for a list of the scenes, the current one marked; tap one to go there. On the Mac: Cmd-Up, Cmd-Down and Cmd-L.

Build 125, 2026-09-22

  • The Mac disk image is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, the first one that is: it opens like any app, with no Open Anyway step, and its Read me first no longer has that section. The 122 image was the last unsigned one.
  • Open PDF with sides already open starts a new packet with the file you pick; the old packet's roster, KEY and notes are remembered the way Close packet remembers them. Add another PDF still adds to the open packet. Before, Open PDF with a packet open added the file to it, or, for the same file, replaced it in place, which looked like nothing happened.

Build 122, 2026-09-22

  • The Mac disk image of this build is unsigned like the ones before it (the Open Anyway step in the Read me first); the signed, notarized one follows when Apple finishes its first look at the app, a day or two for a new developer account.
  • The scene lock is off unless you turn it on: the bar's lock capsule or Display > Follow > Stay in the scene. Once on, it stays on. Build 120 had it on by default; the tester who asked for the lock wanted it the other way, and he is right that the first day with the app should behave as before.

Build 120, 2026-09-22

  • Follow stays in the scene. With the lock on, which is the default, the page cannot be moved to another scene by what the room says; when the room sounds like another scene, a chip in the rail offers it and one tap moves there. A tap on a line, Top of scene or the scene list move the lock with you. The lock capsule in the bar shows the scene number; tap it to follow across scenes, as for a shot that runs from one scene into the next. Display > Follow > Stay in the scene is the same switch.

Build 119, 2026-09-22 (iPad)

  • Pinch the page to size it. Two fingers set the text size of the layout you are in, Screenplay or Reader, live as you pinch, and Display shows the same value. It sticks. With Follow on, the page comes back to the current line when you let go. On the Mac the trackpad pinch does the same.

Build 115, 2026-09-22 (Mac)

  • After Close packet, an Open PDF that fails (a file still in iCloud, a slow disk) now shows its message instead of looking like nothing: the open waits for the panel to close first. The diagnostic report records the Open panel's answer.

Build 113, 2026-09-22

  • Sixteen highlight colors instead of eight: coral, rose, orchid, periwinkle, aqua, lime, gold and tan join the first eight, in the KEY, on the page and in the export PDF, and in the free web tool. The four highlighter colors still come first. There is no gray on purpose: a gray highlight looks like the shading sides use for omitted text.

Build 110, 2026-09-22

  • Follow always turns off. Before, a tap on Follow was ignored while the speech model was still loading after a turn-on, which on an iPad's first run is a minute; the button now says "loading the speech model" during that time and a tap turns Follow off at any moment.
  • On the Mac, Open PDF, Add another PDF and Cmd-O use the Mac's own Open panel. Before, after Close packet, Open PDF could show no window at all.
  • A tester log: every report, user error included, is written down with what it pointed to and what changed.

Build 107, 2026-09-22

  • Settings has a User manual link that opens the manual in the browser. The manual itself is at https://gotham-sound.github.io/sides-helper-site/manual.html, current for both platforms with fresh screens.

Build 104, 2026-09-22

  • The Follow unlock reads $49.99 everywhere, matching the price on the App Store sheet.
  • The build number shows in Settings, About and the diagnostic report; the Mac read-me says it too.
  • A PDF opened again replaces its copy in the packet instead of being added twice. Notes re-attach and the cursor stays.
  • A slow line is given its own time: once enough of a line has been heard, that line's own pace sets how long the rest is expected to take when it says the line runs slower than average, so the next name lights later on a slow line rather than early.

Build 100, 2026-09-21

  • The audio input on the Mac is captured through an audio unit pinned to the device you pick. Before, picking any device whose sample rate differed from the Mac's default input captured nothing, silently, and a stereo device beside a mono default gave channel 1 only. Devices are remembered by a stable identifier, so a pick survives re-plugging and a reboot.
  • Only an input you picked in Feed + STT check is remembered. With nothing picked, a recorder over USB is used when one is plugged in, otherwise the built-in mic, and that guess is never written down as your choice. A pick that is unplugged when Follow turns on is named in an alert; another feed stands in if one is there, the built-in mic never does.
  • Sum in the channel picker is channels 1 and 2 only. Before, it averaged every channel the device offered.
  • The text under the macOS microphone prompt says the input you pick, USB feed included, is what is used.

Build 97, 2026-09-21

  • The first open after the app had sat idle for a few seconds no longer hangs on the Mac: the sides engine's web view lives in the window, where macOS does not suspend it.
  • An open that stalls says so with a message after a set time instead of spinning forever, and the diagnostic report carries the engine's own log.
  • Settings, and with it Send a report, are reachable before any sides are open: on the first screen, and on the Mac under the app menu.

Build 91, 2026-09-21

  • The first TestFlight build. The sides tool, the KEY, Follow with the Page, Drum, Horizon and Picture displays, notes and flags, Print / Export, the sample sides, Open a sides link, Send a report.
